Health and Safety

ScaleTrack is a personal wellness journal. It is not intended for medical use or as a health care service. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

Check every reading

Camera and screenshot recognition can be wrong. Confirm each number before saving it. Do not use ScaleTrack for urgent decisions or to make medication, treatment, nutrition, or exercise changes.

Estimates have limits

Trends, pace, goal dates, and BMI are mathematical estimates based on limited information. They can be incomplete or inaccurate. BMI is an adult screening estimate and may not be appropriate for children, pregnancy, athletes, or every body.

Use goals with care

A displayed goal or past pace is not a recommended or safe target. If tracking weight causes distress, encourages unsafe restriction, or conflicts with professional advice, stop using those features and seek qualified support.

Emergencies

ScaleTrack cannot provide emergency help. If you believe you have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency service.
