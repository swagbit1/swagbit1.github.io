Student Performance Predictor

Comparing models that predict 12 letter-grade categories from student data.

PythonPandasscikit-learnTensorFlowNumPyMatplotlib

Weighted F1 improved from 0.619 to 0.648.

The problem

Predicting student outcomes requires more than an accuracy score. The project explored how model choice behaves across an imbalanced set of grade categories.

My contribution

Merged and cleaned 86,000 records from three sources, selected 15 academic and lifestyle features, and trained Logistic Regression and a 173,964-parameter TensorFlow network using a stratified 80/20 split.

Engineering decisions

Compare a baseline

Logistic Regression provides a reference for the neural network rather than treating complexity as success.

Look beyond accuracy

Accuracy stayed around 65.8%, while weighted F1 increased from about 0.619 to 0.648.

Inspect dimensionality

On a 10,000-record sample, 13 of 15 principal components captured 95% of observed variance.

Evidence

The archived notebook reports the dataset size, model parameters, and evaluation results. A+ accounts for roughly 57.3% of examples; the sources are heterogeneous and possibly synthetic-like.

Source: supplied career archive. Historical measurements are identified as such.

Lessons and limitations

The notebook appears to standardize before splitting, creating a leakage concern. The next correction is to split first, fit preprocessing only on training data, and rerun evaluation. The reported results should be read with that limitation.
