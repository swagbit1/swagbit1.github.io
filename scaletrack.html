ScaleTrack

An iPhone weight tracker with photo entry and local storage.

SwiftUISwiftDataVision OCRHealthKit
App StorePublished iPhone app

Independent product development and release.

The problem

Daily weight logging should be quick and useful without a separate account or a remote service for the core workflow.

My contribution

Designed and built the native SwiftUI application, its SwiftData persistence, trends, goals, progress photos, and export. Integrated Vision OCR and optional HealthKit synchronization, then released the app through App Review.

Engineering decisions

Local core

SwiftData stores tracking history on the device, keeping core logging independent of a backend.

Optional integrations

Vision OCR adds photo-based entry; HealthKit adds a permissioned synchronization path.

Release iteration

Resolved two App Review metadata rejections before release, treating distribution as part of delivering the product.

Evidence

Published on the Apple App Store. The supplied résumé reports 1.41K impressions, 112 product-page views, 43 first-time downloads, and a 4.41% conversion rate without advertising. These are reported historical figures, not live analytics.

Source: supplied career archive. Historical measurements are identified as such.

Lessons and limitations

Useful integrations should support the core experience. Keeping them optional makes the main workflow easier to understand and less dependent on external services.
