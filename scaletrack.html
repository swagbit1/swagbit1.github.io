CASE STUDY // 2026

ScaleTrack

A local-first iPhone weight tracker built to make daily logging fast while keeping the core experience useful without an account or a remote backend.

SwiftUI + SwiftDataNative interface and local data model for the core tracking workflow.
Vision OCROptional photo-based entry that reads a scale display and lets the user confirm the result.
HealthKitOptional synchronization that extends the app without becoming a requirement.

THE PROBLEM

Weight tracking should not require an account, a subscription, or a slow multi-step workflow. ScaleTrack focuses on the core job: recording a weigh-in, understanding a trend, and staying aware of progress toward a goal.

THE BUILD

Record. Users can add a weight manually or begin from a photo of a scale display.
Understand. Locally stored history drives trends, goals, progress photos, and export.
Extend by choice. Vision OCR and HealthKit are optional input and sync paths, not dependencies for daily tracking.

DESIGN CHOICE

The most important decision was local-first design. The app remains useful with no account and no separate service. That keeps the primary workflow direct and makes external integrations deliberate opt-in enhancements rather than points of failure.
