SafeBite

An ingredient-label scanner that checks text against allergen and dietary preferences.

SwiftUIVision OCRSwiftData

How it works

Vision OCR reads the ingredient label. A Swift parser and matcher evaluate the text against allergen and dietary preferences. Uncertain labels fall back to Caution.

Design decision

The deterministic safety verdict is separate from optional AI explanation text. An explanation cannot change the underlying result.

Status

Device testing and App Store upload were pending in the archived handoff.
