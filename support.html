ScaleTrack Support

For help with ScaleTrack, email shinninglegend15@gmail.com.

Before you write

  • Include your iOS version and ScaleTrack version from the bottom of Settings.
  • Describe what you expected and what happened.
  • You may attach the diagnostic file created in Settings. It excludes readings, measurements, notes, tags, and photos.
  • Do not send progress photos or health records unless they are necessary and you intend to share them.

Data controls

Settings includes CSV export, backup and restore, Delete Everything, and a separate option to remove Apple Health samples created by ScaleTrack.

Important limitation

ScaleTrack is a general wellness journal. It is not intended for medical use or as an emergency service and does not provide medical advice.
